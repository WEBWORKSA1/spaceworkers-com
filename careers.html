Role families

Career paths

💻

Flight & Ground Software

Real-time embedded code, fault protection, simulation and mission-control software.

Learn: C/C++, Rust, Python, RTOS, Linux, cFS/F Prime.

🧭

Systems, GNC & Mission Design

Requirements, interfaces, trade studies, orbits and attitude control.

Learn: MATLAB/Simulink, STK/GMAT, MBSE (SysML), control theory.

🔥

Propulsion

Liquid, solid and electric engines — design, test stands and combustion analysis.

Learn: thermodynamics, CFD, test data analysis, cryogenics.

📡

RF, Avionics & Power

Antennas, radios, link budgets, harnesses, batteries and solar arrays.

Learn: RF design, PCB layout, SDR, EMC testing.

🏗️

Mechanical & Structures

Structures, thermal, mechanisms and composites that survive launch and vacuum.

Learn: CAD, FEA, GD&T, thermal analysis.

🔧

Technicians & Manufacturing

Build, integrate and test flight hardware. Certificates and apprenticeships count.

Learn: IPC J-STD soldering, composites, welding, NDT.

🛰️

Mission & Satellite Operations

Flight control, anomaly response, ground stations and constellation management.

Learn: telemetry tools, orbital mechanics basics, scripting.

🌍

Data, AI & Geospatial

Satellite imagery analytics, ML pipelines and space situational awareness.

Learn: Python, GIS, remote sensing, computer vision.

⚖️

Policy, Law & Business

Licensing, spectrum, export control, contracts, BD, finance and program management.

Learn: ITAR/EAR, FAA/FCC processes, government contracting.

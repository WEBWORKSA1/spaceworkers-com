FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SpaceWorkers free for job seekers?

Yes. Searching employers, reading guides, watching videos, entering contests and joining the talent network are all free.

How do employers work with SpaceWorkers?

Employers can post roles, buy featured placements, sponsor content or contests, or use our shortlist service. See Hire Talent for plans.

Do I need to be an engineer to work in space?

No. Technicians, software developers, data analysts, supply-chain, finance, legal, HR and marketing professionals are all hired in large numbers.

Where does donation money go?

To free career resources, scholarships and contest prize pools, plus hosting and operations. See the allocation breakdown on the Support page.
